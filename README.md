# FixNest
## Problem Statement
In today's technology-driven world, people heavily depend on electronic devices and machinery such as smartphones, laptops, computers, home appliances and industrial equipment. However, when these devices or machines malfunction, customers face difficulties finding skilled and trustworthy repair technicians.

There is no convenient centralized platform where users can discover verified service providers, compare repair costs, check technician expertise, verify spare-part authenticity and track repair progress. As a result, customers may experience overcharging, poor-quality repairs, unnecessary delays and a lack of service transparency.

Therefore, there is a need for a centralized digital platform that connects customers with verified repair professionals, provides transparent pricing, enables service booking and tracking, and improves the overall repair experience through reliable and accessible services.
